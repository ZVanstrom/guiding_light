Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Guiding Hope Resource Center ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email address, the topic you choose, and your message. Our contact form is processed by Formspree.
  • When you buy something: your name, email address, and payment details. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • When you visit the site: like most websites, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may also receive your IP address.

We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions
  • To deliver products you purchase, such as emailing your workbook
  • To provide receipts and customer support
  • To keep records required by law, such as for taxes

We will not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

How we share your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above, and only so they can do their jobs for us, or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and order records only as long as needed to help you and to meet legal and tax requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct, or delete the personal information we have about you. Contact us through the contact form on our website.

Children

This website is meant for adults, including parents, caregivers, and adult self-advocates. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Contact us through the contact form on our website or email [business email].
