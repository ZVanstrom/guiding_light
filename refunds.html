Refund Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

How delivery works

Our workbooks and guides are digital (PDF) products. After you buy, we email your file to the address you entered at checkout within 1 business day.

If it hasn't arrived after 1 business day, please check your spam or junk folder, then contact us through the contact form on our website. We'll send it again right away.

Refunds

Because digital files can't be returned, all sales are final once the file has been delivered. We will gladly give a full refund if:

  • You did not receive your file within 3 business days and we can't resolve it.
  • You were charged more than once for the same item.
  • The file is damaged or won't open, and a replacement doesn't fix it.

If something else went wrong, please reach out anyway. We want you to be happy with your purchase.

How to ask for a refund

Contact us through the contact form on our website within 30 days of your purchase. Include the name and email address you used at checkout.

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Depending on your bank, it can take 5–10 business days to show up.

Questions

Contact us through the contact form on our website or email [business email].
