Resources. Guidance. Support.

Helping families go from school services to adult life.

Moving from school to adulthood can feel overwhelming for people with intellectual disabilities, autism, and their families. Many families reach the end of the school years and ask:

“What do we do now?”

Guiding Hope Resource Center gives you clear information, practical guidance, and simple tools to help you feel ready for what comes next.

You have options. You have resources.
You are not alone.

Explore Free Resources Visit the Resource Shop

You shouldn't have to start from scratch.

For years, the school's IEP gave your family a roadmap. Then graduation gets close, and the roadmap changes. Families often wonder:

  • What happens after high school?
  • What services are available?
  • What programs might be a good fit?
  • What should we be asking?
  • Where do we start?
  • Who can help?
  • What if the first option doesn't work?

We give you easy-to-understand information and practical tools so you can take the next step with more confidence.

Why I Created Guiding Hope

I have spent 24 years working in the field of intellectual disabilities and autism, alongside individuals, families, and professionals. I've seen both the challenges and the possibilities that come with finding services and supports.

Again and again, I've seen how hard the move from school to adulthood can be. After years of an IEP and a school team guiding the way, families suddenly find themselves asking, “What happens next?”

I've met families who didn't know what resources were available, or where to start looking for programs that could help their loved one begin adult life.

That is why I created Guiding Hope Resource Center: to make this transition feel less overwhelming, easier to understand, and more comfortable for families.

There are options. There are resources.
And you are not alone.

Important Disclaimer

Guiding Hope Resource Center provides educational information and practical resources to help individuals and families better understand available services and supports.

This information is not legal, medical, financial, or individualized professional advice. Services, eligibility requirements, programs, and resources can change. Please check current information with the right agency, service coordinator, provider, or other qualified professional.

Our goal is to help families become more informed and prepared, not to make decisions for them.
