Questions Families Should Ask

Questions you can bring to calls and meetings. Check off the ones that matter to you.

Getting started

  • What services might we be eligible for?
  • How do we apply?
  • Is there a waiting list?
  • Who do I call with questions?
  • What happens next, and when?

Choosing a provider

  • What services do you offer?
  • Do you have openings?
  • How do you share updates with families?
  • Who do I contact if there is a problem?

At a planning meeting

  • Does this plan include my loved one's goals and choices?
  • Who is responsible for each part of the plan?
  • When will we review the plan?
  • How do we ask for changes?

When something isn't working

  • Who should I talk to first?
  • What are our options?
  • How do I share a concern?
