Understanding Adult Services

Plain-language meanings for words you may hear.

Every state is different. Names, services, and rules can change depending on where you live. Always ask your local team what applies to you.

Words you may hear

IEP
Individualized Education Program. A plan for special education services at school.
ISP
Individual Support Plan. A plan about a person's life, goals, choices, and the support they need. See our ISP Meeting Guide.
Coordinator
A person who may help plan and organize services. The job title is different in different places. You may hear "supports coordinator," "service coordinator," or "case manager."
Eligibility
The rules that decide who can get a service.
Provider
An agency or person that gives a service.
Waiver
A kind of program that may pay for some services. Ask your team what programs are available where you live.
Waiting list
Some services have a wait. Ask if there is one, and how to get on it.
Respite
Short-term care that gives family caregivers a break.

Tip

If you don't understand a word, it's okay to ask:

"Can you explain that in a different way?"
