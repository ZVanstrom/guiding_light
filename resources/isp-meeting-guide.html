What to Expect at an ISP Meeting

A simple guide to help you get ready. Free to download and print, written in Easy Read.

What is an ISP meeting?

ISP means Individual Support Plan. An ISP meeting is a time to talk about:

  • My life
  • My goals
  • My choices
  • The support I need

This meeting is about you. You can ask questions. You can ask someone to explain something in a different way. Your thoughts and your choices are important.

What is in this guide?

The guide has 5 short parts:

  1. Learn about my meeting: what an ISP meeting is and who will be there.
  2. Get ready: simple ways to prepare before the meeting.
  3. Questions I can ask: ideas for questions to bring.
  4. My meeting notes: space to write down what happens.
  5. After my meeting: what to do next.

It is written in Easy Read, so people with intellectual disabilities or autism can use it on their own or with a family member or support person.
