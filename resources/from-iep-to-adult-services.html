From IEP to Adult Services

A short guide to help you start planning for life after school.

What changes?

At school, the IEP team helps plan services.

When school ends, the IEP ends too.

Adult services work differently. They may have their own applications, rules, and wait times.

Every state is different. Names, services, and rules can change depending on where you live. Always ask your local team what applies to you.

Start early

You don't have to have all the answers. A good first step is to ask questions while school services are still in place.

Checklist: getting started

  • Ask the IEP team about transition planning.
  • Ask who to contact about adult services where you live.
  • Ask how to apply, and what papers you will need.
  • Gather important papers in one place.
  • Write down goals for adult life: work, learning, home, and community.
  • Keep a list of names, phone numbers, and dates.

Questions to ask the school team

  • When will school services end?
  • What transition services are in the IEP?
  • Who can help us connect with adult services?
  • What should we do before school ends?
